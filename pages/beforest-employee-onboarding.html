ABOUT OUR COMPANY

Beforest was founded in 2018, with our headquarters located in Hyderabad. We focus on creating food and water-secure landscapes by applying permaculture principles and natural farming techniques. We bring together like-minded individuals passionate about sustainable agriculture, helping them collectively purchase large estates. From planning and execution to ongoing management, we oversee the estate on behalf of our member collective.

THE BEFOREST PROCESS

Restoring nature. Rebuilding future.

  1. IDENTIFY

    Identify, evaluate, acquire abandoned landscapes.

  2. RESEARCH

    Carry out imaging, soil testing, and research on the history of land.

  3. MAP

    Map water courses, aquifers and reservoirs. If none, create them.

  4. COLLABORATE

    Bring in collective members, and in construction of earth homes.

  5. CREATE ZONES

    Create zones for food forests, wilderness and human habitation.

  6. STUDY & REWILD

    Study the natural flora and fauna of the landscape, and rewild.

  7. GROW & MARKET

    Grow and market forest friendly produce appropriately.

  8. EXIT & HAND OVER

    Exit and hand over the landscape.

Together, we heal the earth and build a legacy.
