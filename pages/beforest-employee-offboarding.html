Employee Exit Process

Offboarding SOP

Ensuring every departure is handled with proper communication, documentation, knowledge transfer, access closure, asset recovery, and final settlement.

Why this process exists

Purpose & Scope

  1. 01

    The purpose of this SOP is to outline the steps to be followed when an employee exits the organization, ensuring a structured, professional, and consistent offboarding process.

  2. 02

    This helps mitigate risks, secures company property, and ensures that knowledge transfer and compliance requirements are addressed.

  3. 03

    This procedure applies to all employees (full-time, part-time, temporary, or contract) who are leaving the company voluntarily or involuntarily.

Offboarding SOP

Responsibilities

  1. 01

    HR Manager

    Oversees the entire offboarding process, conducts exit interview, and handles final documentation.

  2. 02

    Hiring Manager/Supervisor

    Ensures knowledge transfer, final task completion, and transition of responsibilities.

  1. 03

    IT Department

    Removes access to systems/tools, retrieves company devices, and deactivates email/cloud/software access.

  2. 04

    Admin/Facilities Team

    Collects company property (ID cards, office keys, etc.) and prepares workspace for next hire.

  3. 05

    Employee

    Completes exit formalities, such as returning property and participating in knowledge transfer.

Employee Notification (Upon Resignation or Termination)

Procedure

  1. 01

    Employee gives notice of resignation or is notified of termination, in accordance with company policy.

  2. 02

    HR acknowledges the resignation or termination and informs relevant departments (IT, Admin, Hiring Manager).

  3. 03

    Exit Date is agreed upon between HR and the employee, with consideration for the notice period.

Company Property Return & IT Access Closure

Return & Access

Admin/Facilities Team

Admin/Facilities Team ensures that the employee returns all company-owned property, including:

  • Laptop, phone, or any other devices
  • Office keys, ID badges, or access cards
  • Company documents (physical and digital files)

IT Department

IT Department:

  • Deactivates access to company systems, including:
    • Email
    • File-sharing platforms (Google Drive, SharePoint, etc.)
    • Internal software tools (CRM, HRMS, etc.)
  • Retrieves any company-owned hardware, such as laptops or mobile devices.
  • Resets passwords for systems where necessary.

Last Week of Employment

Final Payroll and Benefits Settlement

Final Pay

HR processes final pay, ensuring:

  • Calculation of final working days
  • Unused leave or vacation days (if applicable)
  • Any outstanding dues or bonuses

Benefits Status

HR informs the employee about the status of their benefits:

  • Pension or retirement funds
  • Health insurance coverage
  • Any pending severance or compensation (for terminated employees)

On the Last Working Day

Exit Interview

HR conducts the exit interview:

  1. Gather feedback on work experience, team dynamics, and reasons for leaving.

  2. Identify areas of improvement for the company’s HR practices and work environment.

  3. Address any final questions the employee might have regarding their exit.

HR documents the key findings from the exit interview and shares it with relevant departments (management, HR leadership).

Last Day

Final Documentation

  1. 01

    Relieving letter or Experience certificate (if applicable)

  2. 02

    Full & Final settlement letter

  3. 03

    Non-disclosure agreement (if applicable)

  4. 04

    Tax-related documents (form 16, etc.)

Last Day

Off-boarding Checklist
Completion

HR ensures that all offboarding formalities are complete, including:

  1. 01

    Verification of returned company property

  2. 02

    Finalized payroll and benefits settlement

  3. 03

    Exit interview completed and feedback recorded

  4. 04

    Systems access revoked

Employee

Employee’s Departure

  1. 01

    Completes any final paperwork (HR-related forms, acknowledgment of return of property, Exit survey, etc.).

  2. 02

    Exits the company on good terms, with all formalities completed.

Offboarding SOP

Action Items
