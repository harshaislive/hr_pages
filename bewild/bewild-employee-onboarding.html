About Our Company

Founded in 2018, Bewild was created with a vision to redefine the way people experience food—by making it wholesome, sustainable, and deeply connected to nature.

As part of the Be-Ecoverse ecosystem, Bewild believes that food should not only nourish people but also regenerate the environment and strengthen local communities.

We work closely with farmers, producers, and local partners to promote responsible sourcing, sustainable practices, and mindful consumption. Every product and experience we create reflects our commitment to quality, transparency, and positive impact.

At Bewild, we are building more than a food brand—we are cultivating a movement that connects people with nature through food while creating lasting value for individuals, communities, and the planet.

The Story of Our Produce / 01

From Soil to Seed

We, the Bewild produce, come to life through our community's efforts in enriching soil and supporting their economic well-being.

Stages 01-06
  1. 01

    Zoning & Seed Selection

    After a biodiversity survey, our community identifies cultivation and wilderness areas on our 100+ acre landscape. They select suitable native varieties to grow.

  2. 02

    Green Manuring

    Forty-five days before sowing, the soil is enriched with green manuring, ensuring it's ready to support our growth.

  3. 03

    Seed Sourcing

    Our seeds are procured from a community of farmers and seed savers, ensuring non-GMO, native, heirloom varieties.

  4. 04

    Land Preparation

    1-3 days before sowing, the land is gently tilled and enriched with natural nutrients to create the perfect environment for us.

  5. 05

    Sowing & Transplanting

    With hope, we are sown into the soil or transplanted from the nursery, ready to grow and flourish.

  6. 06

    Early Nourishment

    We receive extra care with cow dung, biomass, water, and mulch to protect us from harsh weather and ensure strong roots.

The Story of Our Produce / 02

From Growth to You

From careful maintenance to your honest feedback, every stage protects the freshness, quality, and integrity of what we grow.

Stages 07-12
  1. 07

    Maintenance

    Regular pruning, weeding, and natural pest control keep us healthy and resilient as we grow.

  2. 08

    Harvesting

    At peak ripeness, we are handpicked and checked for quality, maintaining our freshness and integrity.

  3. 09

    Seed Saving

    A portion of us is saved to build an in-house seed bank, preserving our lineage for future generations.

  4. 10

    Post Harvesting

    We are thoroughly sorted, cleaned, and processed to bring out our best qualities.

  5. 11

    Bewild Produce & Products

    Forest-friendly wellness, delivered to you directly, reducing up to 50% of the food waste compared to the market standard.

  6. 12

    Feedback From You

    Share your honest thoughts about our produce and products on our social media channels, and we'll work on improving our processes to offer you only the best.
