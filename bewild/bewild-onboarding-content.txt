Slide 4: About Our Company

Founded in 2018, Bewild was created with a vision to redefine the way people experience food—by making it wholesome, sustainable, and deeply connected to nature.

As part of the Be-Ecoverse ecosystem, Bewild believes that food should not only nourish people but also regenerate the environment and strengthen local communities.

We work closely with farmers, producers, and local partners to promote responsible sourcing, sustainable practices, and mindful consumption. Every product and experience we create reflects our commitment to quality, transparency, and positive impact.

At Bewild, we are building more than a food brand—we are cultivating a movement that connects people with nature through food while creating lasting value for individuals, communities, and the planet.
